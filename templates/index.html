How It Works
1
Document Extraction

Parses text from PDF (PyMuPDF / pypdf), Word (.docx tables and text), or TXT with encoding detection.

2
Legal Preprocessing

Protects legal abbreviations (v., sec., art., inc.), preserves negations (not, never) and conditions.

3
Extractive Summarization

Ranks sentences using TextRank or TF-IDF Cosine Centroid with legal obligation weighting. Prevents hallucination.

4
Clause & Entity Mining

Identifies Termination, Confidentiality, Liabilities, Monetary sums, Dates, and Obligations.

Key Academic Feature

Extractive vs. Abstractive: Unlike LLM generators which can hallucinate legal terms, extractive NLP selects verbatim sentences, maintaining 100% legal traceability.
